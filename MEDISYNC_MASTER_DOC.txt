## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
MediSync is a production-grade healthcare ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between doctor prescriptions and patient medication adherence. It integrates mobile applications, a robust FastAPI backend, state-of-the-art AI (Gemini & Groq), and IoT hardware to ensure patients never miss a dose.

---

## 2. THE PROBLEM STATEMENT
- **Handwriting Issues:** Patients struggle to read messy handwritten prescriptions.
- **Non-Adherence:** Forgetting doses leads to chronic health complications.
- **Complexity:** Elderly patients find traditional healthcare apps difficult.
- **Doctor Blindness:** Doctors lack visibility into whether patients follow treatments.

---

## 3. COMPREHENSIVE TECH STACK

### A. Mobile Application (Patient & Doctor)
- **Framework:** React Native with Expo (SDK 54)
- **Language:** JavaScript / TypeScript
- **State Management:** Zustand
- **Navigation:** React Navigation (Bottom Tabs, Native Stack)
- **Styling:** NativeWind (Tailwind CSS)
- **Local Storage:** MMKV (High-speed), SQLite, SecureStore
- **Notifications:** Notifee, FCM, Expo Notifications
- **Multimedia:** Expo AV (Audio), Expo Speech (TTS), Expo Image Picker (OCR)

### B. Backend Infrastructure
- **Framework:** FastAPI (Python 3.10+)
- **Server:** Uvicorn / Gunicorn
- **Database:** MongoDB (Atlas) & Google Cloud Firestore
- **Auth:** JWT with Passlib bcrypt hashing
- **Scheduling:** APScheduler (Cron jobs for reminders)
- **Observability:** Sentry (Performance & Crash monitoring)

### C. Artificial Intelligence Pipeline
- **Vision & OCR:** Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (Prescription parsing)
- **Conversational AI:** Groq Llama-3 70B (Fast, contextual chat)
- **Speech-to-Text:** Groq Whisper (Multilingual support)
- **Fuzzy Matching:** TheFuzz (Ensuring medical terminology accuracy)

### D. Communication & Hardware
- **Voice/SMS:** Twilio API (Automated AI calling)
- **IoT Controller:** ESP8266 / ESP32 (Smart Pillbox integration)
- **Storage:** Google Cloud Storage (Media files)

## 4. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & MODULES

### 4.1. Intelligent Prescription OCR Pipeline
- Users upload a photo of a prescription.
- Gemini Vision AI decodes handwriting and abbreviations (BD, TDS, SOS).
- Normalizes data into medicine names, dosages, timings, and food instructions.
- Extracts "Patient Memory" (allergies, chronic conditions).

### 4.2. Smart Pillbox & Adherence Tracking
- Extracted data builds a daily timeline (Morning, Afternoon, Night).
- Real-time adherence scores calculated based on "Taken" or "Skipped" actions.
- Automatically calculates medicine expiry and depletion dates.

### 4.3. Multilingual AI Voice Assistant
- Speaks English and Hindi natively.
- Transcription via Whisper allows patients to ask questions like "Should I take this on an empty stomach?".
- Context-aware: AI knows the patient's full medical history.

### 4.4. AI Calling & Escalation Engine (New)
A 4-tier Intelligent Escalation Logic:
1. **15 Mins Late:** Standard Push Notification.
2. **30 Mins Late:** Urgent Push Notification.
3. **60 Mins Late:** Automated AI Phone Call (Twilio) speaking the user's name and medicine.
4. **Caregiver Alert:** SMS to emergency contact if 3+ doses are missed.

### 4.5. Doctor-Patient Clinical Bridge
- Dedicated `DoctorNotificationsScreen` for monitoring patient alerts.
- Web Dashboard for doctors to view holistic "Adherence Reports".
- Direct secure messaging between doctor and patient.

## 5. USER JOURNEYS

### [Patient Journey]
1. Register -> Upload Prescription -> AI builds Schedule.
2. Receive Notification -> Mark as Taken.
3. Ask AI via Voice: "Should I stop taking these meds if I feel dizzy?" -> AI checks history and advises.

### [Doctor Journey]
1. Log into Dashboard -> View Patient Roster.
2. Notice low adherence (e.g., 40%) -> Intervene via secure message.
3. Review AI-parsed prescriptions to verify accuracy.
